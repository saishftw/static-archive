INDIA: 75 YEARS OF
GOVERNMENT ECONOMIC PERFORMANCE

Eleven macroeconomic indicators compared across nine Indian governments. Growth series are compound annual growth rates (CAGR) over each term; share and level series are term averages. Source figures as compiled from World Bank data on Past Performance — AndhBhakt.org.

Dashes (—) mark indicators with no recorded observation for that term and are excluded from every chart and ranking.

1 / MASTER CROSS-METRIC HEATMAP

Cells show the exact recorded figure. Colour encodes the value normalised within each indicator (0 = weakest government on that metric, 1 = strongest). Average Inflation and Debt/GDP are inverted, so lower values read green. Greener is always better.

2 / BY METRIC DIRECTION

HIGHER IS BETTER — GROWTH RATES (CAGR %)

Real GDP, GDP per capita, government revenue, manufacturing, services and stock market.

HIGHER IS BETTER — OPENNESS & INVESTMENT (% OF GDP)

Exports, imports and FDI as a share of GDP. Plotted separately because these are levels, not growth rates.

LOWER IS BETTER — INFLATION & DEBT (%)

Average inflation and average debt-to-GDP. Shorter bars indicate stronger performance.

3 / MODERN ERA BREAKDOWN

Vajpayee (1998–04), Manmohan Singh (2004–14) and Modi (2014–25) have near-complete observations across all eleven indicators, allowing a direct like-for-like comparison. The radar view normalises each axis within these three governments only (outer edge = best of the three, with inflation and debt inverted).

4 / COMPLETE DATA REFERENCE

Figures reproduced as recorded; no interpolation or estimation has been applied to missing values.
